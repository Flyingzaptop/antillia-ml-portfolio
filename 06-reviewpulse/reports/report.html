01 / Experiment design · Metodyka

Frozen multilingual MiniLM encoder with trained MLP; baseline TF-IDF logistic regression. CPU, 2 threads, seed 42. Normalized exact duplicates removed before partitioning. Preprocessing fitted only on train. Model and alpha selected by validation macro F1. Test is reserved for final evaluation.

Train 1,906 · Validation 477 · Test 596. Selected model: embedding_mlp. Human-review threshold: 0.850, selected solely on validation to target 95% selective accuracy with at least 15% coverage; otherwise best selective accuracy at that coverage.

07 / Limits & reproducibility · Ograniczenia

Scores are uncalibrated classifier estimates. No reliable out-of-domain detection. Polish transfer is not proven beyond the tiny diagnostic. Pretraining-data overlap cannot be ruled out. Binary sentiment only; no neutral class; sarcasm and mixed sentiment are not reliably handled. These are project-owned measurements, not external certification.

MLP optimizer may reach its 160-epoch cap; the actual iteration count is recorded below. Results are retained even when a neural model does not outperform baseline. Run evaluate.ps1 to reproduce metrics using saved artifacts. Checksums, split manifest, model weights and JSON/CSV results are shipped with the project.
