Design uses the original antillia.tech site.css, its Manrope/DM Sans fonts and Antillia mark. Font licenses are in case-study-assets/fonts/. Diagrams are original SVG, model visualizations derive from saved artifacts, and screenshots are actual app captures. PL/EN is switched locally without external APIs. These files are prepared for later integration into antillia.tech; production site has not been modified.
